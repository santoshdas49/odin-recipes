Description

If you're looking for a homemade pizza crust recipe that's great for beginners, you're in luck. This top-rated recipe is super easy to throw together on a whim – and it puts the store-bought stuff to shame. Learn how to make the best pizza crust of your life with just a few ingredients, find out how to shape the dough, and get our best storage secrets.

Pizza Crust Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tbsp minced garlic
  • 8 Roma tomatoes,sliced
  • 2(12 inch) pre-baked pizza crusts
  • 8 ounces shredded Mozzarella cheese
  • 4 ounces shredded Fontina cheese
  • 10 fresh basil leaves,washed,dried
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese

Directions

  1. Step 1:Stir together olive oil, garlic, and salt; toss with tomatoes, and allow to stand for 15 minutes. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C).
  2. Step 2:Brush each pizza crust with some of the tomato marinade. Sprinkle the pizzas evenly with Mozzarella and Fontina cheeses. Arrange tomatoes overtop, then sprinkle with shredded basil, Parmesan, and feta cheese.
  3. Step 3:Bake in preheated oven until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown, about 10 minutes.

Enjoy!
